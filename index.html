About

I am an Assistant Professor in the Leslie A. Rose Department of Mechanical Engineering at South Dakota Mines. My work focuses on thermal-fluid sciences, thermal management, energy systems, and sustainable thermal technologies.

Research

My current research interests include:

Thermal Management Cooling and heat-transfer technologies for engineering systems.
Phase Change Materials Thermal energy storage and temperature-control applications.
Waste Heat Recovery Recovery and utilization of low-grade thermal energy.
Sustainable Energy Systems Energy-efficient thermal systems and experimental research.

Teaching

My teaching interests include thermodynamics, thermal-fluid sciences, engineering design, and experimental methods in mechanical engineering.
