About

I am an Assistant Professor in the Leslie A. Rose Department of Mechanical Engineering at South Dakota Mines. My research focuses on thermal-fluid sciences, thermal management, energy systems, and sustainable thermal technologies.

Research

My research interests include:

  • Thermal-fluid sciences
  • Thermal management
  • Phase change materials and thermal energy storage
  • Waste heat recovery
  • Energy-efficient cooling technologies
  • Sustainable energy systems

Teaching

My teaching interests include thermodynamics, thermal-fluid sciences, engineering design, and experimental methods in mechanical engineering.
