About

I am an Assistant Professor in the Leslie A. Rose Department of Mechanical Engineering at the South Dakota School of Mines and Technology. My research focuses on thermo-fluid science, thermal management, energy systems, and sustainable thermal technologies.

Research

Thermal Management Heat-transfer and cooling technologies for engineering systems.
Phase Change Materials Thermal energy storage and temperature-control applications.
Waste Heat Recovery Recovery and utilization of low-grade thermal energy.
Sustainable Energy Systems Energy-efficient thermal systems and experimental research.
